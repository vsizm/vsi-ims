# VSI Directorate Operating Model

VSI IMS uses a lean seven-function Directorate structure.

## Core organisational engines

- `PROG` — Directorate of Programmes
- `CPRM` — Directorate of Communications, Partnerships & Resource Mobilisation

Programmes is the primary delivery engine. CPRM is a strategic engine for communications, partnerships, fundraising, donor relations and resource mobilisation.

## Enabling and support functions

- `PAR` — Directorate of Policy, Advocacy & Research
- `MEAL` — Directorate of Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning
- `FIN_ADMIN` — Directorate of Finance & Administration
- `LEGAL_COMP` — Directorate of Legal & Compliance
- `OPS` — Directorate of Operations & Field Delivery

These functions enable, measure, govern, protect and support programme delivery rather than being treated as equal programme-delivery silos.

The V1 hierarchy remains:

`Directorate → Programme → Project → Activity`

Legacy thematic Directorate records are retained for historical/audit integrity but are inactive and are excluded from the operational IMS Directorate list.
